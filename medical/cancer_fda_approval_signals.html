About this report: The Cancer FDA Approval Signals report is an automated weekly briefing covering FDA and EMA regulatory decisions for cancer drugs globally. It surfaces new approvals, accelerated approvals, breakthrough therapy designations, label expansions, and rejections across all cancer types. When an approval is granted, a treatment becomes immediately accessible to patients. When a rejection is issued, it signals a setback for patients awaiting that option.
