CT Colonography Analysis

TCIA ACRIN 6664 · National CT Colonography Trial · Generated 2026-06-23 01:11 UTC
Key Findings
  • 70% of patients had no recorded polyp — 243 of 346 patients were CTC-negative for lesions ≥6mm.
  • 115 clinically reportable lesions (≥6mm) remained after deduplication and quality filtering.
  • Large lesions cluster proximally — ≥10mm cohort: cecum + ascending = 47.6%. Medium lesions cluster distally — 6–9mm cohort: sigmoid + rectum = 43.8%.
  • Polypoid morphology predominates — 71.3% of reportable lesions across both cohorts.
  • Siemens scanners generated 58.7% of imaging series (2,025 of 3,451).
Note on cohort count vs size count: The ≥10mm cohort shows 42 reportable lesions but only 38 measure ≥10mm by size. The 4-lesion gap is 6–9mm co-findings recorded in ≥10mm-cohort patients alongside their qualifying large lesion. Cohort assignment reflects enrollment criteria; size bin reflects measured lesion size.
Data quality notes (v1.4.0):
Deduplication: 25 duplicate lesion slots removed (e.g. patient 0495 had slots 8–16 as 9 identical copies of one 2mm rectal finding).
Duplicate patient: 1 patient (0216) appeared in both files — large-file record kept, medium-file entry removed.
Sub-6mm lesions: 93 sub-6mm findings are incidental recordings in large-cohort patients — excluded from primary distributions below.
Primary metric: clinically reportable lesions (≥6mm), consistent with ACRIN 6664 threshold.

Why 3,451 series for ~350 patients?
Each patient had two acquisitions: supine + prone.
Each acquisition generated multiple reconstruction series:
soft-tissue window · bone window · lung window · scout topogram.
2 positions × ~5–8 series/position × 347 patients ≈ 3,500 series.
The 3,451 count is consistent — no data leakage.
