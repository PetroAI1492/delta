Calendar Year 2023

What this report shows: The 20 drugs that cost Medicare Part D the most in 2023 — ranked by total gross drug cost including Medicare, plan, and beneficiary payments. Figures do not reflect manufacturer rebates (CMS is prohibited from disclosing rebate amounts).

Why Part D spending is surging: GLP-1 receptor agonists — originally diabetes drugs, now widely used for weight loss — have driven an extraordinary increase in Part D spending. Ozempic alone went from $0.6B in 2019 to $9.2B in 2023, a 1,564% increase in four years. Medicare Part D does not cover drugs prescribed solely for weight loss — only for FDA-approved indications such as type 2 diabetes.

▪ Spending concentration: The top 5 drugs account for $49.98B — 18.1% of all Part D spending. Five drugs drive nearly 1 in 5 Part D dollars. The top 20 account for $97.80B — 35.4% of total Part D spending.
▪ Diabetes drugs dominate the growth story. GLP-1, SGLT-2, DPP-4, and insulin drugs combined spent $39.35B among the top 20 alone. Ozempic (+1,564%), Jardiance (+511%), and Mounjaro (new in 2022) reflect a fundamental shift in Medicare Part D’s cost structure driven by diabetes and weight-loss drug adoption.
