What this report shows: How NCI cancer research grant money flows to institutions, states, and programs — sourced directly from the US Treasury USASpending.gov API. This is award-level data reported to Congress under the DATA Act.

How this differs from the NCI Fact Book report: The Fact Book shows spending by cancer type (breast, lung, pancreatic). This report shows spending by institution and program (who gets the grants, which states, which programs). The two reports are complementary — not duplicates.

FY2024 data: Covers October 2023 through September 2024. All figures are obligations — funds committed to grantees, not necessarily disbursed.
