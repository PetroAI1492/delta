Data Sources: Influenza-like illness (ILI) surveillance data sourced from the U.S. Outpatient Influenza-like Illness Surveillance Network (ILINet) via the Delphi CMU Epidata API (api.delphi.cmu.edu/epidata/fluview) and stored in the Thor Storehouse (source: 'fluview'). ILI is defined as fever (≥100°F) plus cough and/or sore throat. Weighted ILI (wILI) is state-population-weighted to produce a representative regional and national estimate. Data is updated weekly by CDC on Fridays after 11 AM ET. All data are preliminary and subject to revision. Baselines for the 2025-26 season published by CDC FluView. Percentiles computed against trailing 10-year weekly history.
Disclaimer: All numerical values originate from deterministic, timestamped sources (CDC FluView / ILINet). No AI-generated content.
