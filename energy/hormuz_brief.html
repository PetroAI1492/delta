1.  STRATEGIC OVERVIEW
  • Connects Persian Gulf to Gulf of Oman; ~21 mi wide at narrowest navigable point.
  • ~20-21 MBPD crude/condensate transits (~20-21% of global petroleum traded daily).
  • World's single largest LNG chokepoint; Qatar, UAE, and Kuwait primary exporters.
  • Closure or sustained disruption triggers immediate global energy price shock; no viable alternative route for Gulf producers at comparable volume.
  • Key importers at risk: China, India, Japan, South Korea, EU states.
2.  OPERATIONAL ENVIRONMENT
  • IRAN (IRGCN/IRIN): Maintains fast-attack craft, submarine capability (Kilo-class), anti-ship missiles (Noor/Qader), and mine warfare assets. Routinely conducts freedom-of-navigation harassment operations.

  • Standing Rules: International Recommended Transit Corridor (IRTC) observed; convoy procedures activated in elevated-threat periods.
