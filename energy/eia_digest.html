Methodology
Inventory and flow data from the EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report, published each Wednesday. Natural gas storage from the EIA Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report. Price series from FRED daily and weekly feeds. Week-over-week (WoW) is the difference between the two most recent observations. Year-over-year (YoY) compares the latest value to the closest observation approximately 52 weeks prior. 5-Year percentile ranks the current value against the trailing 260-week distribution.
