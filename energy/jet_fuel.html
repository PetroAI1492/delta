DATA SOURCES
All data sourced directly from Storehouse SQLite database. No AI-generated content, forecasts, or model outputs.

EIA SERIES (Weekly, U.S. Energy Information Administration)
  • PET.WCRIMUS2.W — U.S. Crude Oil Imports (Thousand Barrels/Day)
  • PET.WCESTUS1.W — U.S. Commercial Crude Oil Stocks (Thousand Barrels)
  • PET.WCRRIUS2.W — U.S. Refiner Net Input of Crude Oil (Thousand Barrels/Day)
  • PET.WPULEUS3.W — PADD 3 Refinery Utilization (Percent)
  • PET.WJFSTUS1.W — U.S. Kerosene-Type Jet Fuel Stocks (Thousand Barrels)
  • PET.W_EPJK_YPY_NUS_MBBLD.W — U.S. Refiner Net Production of Kerosene-Type Jet Fuel (Thousand Barrels/Day)
  • PET.WKJIMUS2.W — U.S. Imports of Kerosene-Type Jet Fuel (Thousand Barrels/Day)
  • PET.WKJEXUS2.W — U.S. Exports of Kerosene-Type Jet Fuel (Thousand Barrels/Day)

FRED SERIES (Daily, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis)
  • DJFUELUSGULF — Kerosene-Type Jet Fuel Price: U.S. Gulf Coast ($/Gallon) — aggregated to weekly average

DERIVED METRICS (computed from EIA series, no hardcoded values)
  • Implied Demand (MB/D) = PET.W_EPJK_YPY_NUS_MBBLD.W + PET.WKJIMUS2.W − PET.WKJEXUS2.W − (Stock Change / 7)
  • Days Cover = PET.WJFSTUS1.W ÷ Implied Demand — normal range 20–35 days
  • 4-Week Averages = simple 4-period rolling mean of weekly Implied Demand and Days Cover

METHODOLOGY
Daily jet fuel price aggregated to ISO week average to align with weekly EIA release cadence.
Vertical amber lines mark key market events for reference only — not data annotations.
† First week of series — insufficient prior data to compute stock change.
