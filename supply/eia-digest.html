Methodology
Inventory and flow data from the EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report, published each Wednesday. Natural gas storage from the EIA Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report. Price series from FRED daily and weekly feeds. Week-over-week (WoW) is the difference between the two most recent observations. Year-over-year (YoY) compares the latest value to the closest observation approximately 52 weeks prior. 5-Year percentile ranks the current value against the trailing 260-week distribution. Baker Hughes rig count sourced from the weekly North America Rotary Rig Count report (rigcount.bakerhughes.com), aggregated by country and drill-for type.

All values sourced deterministically from timestamped U.S. government databases. No AI-generated content. EIA data is subject to revision; figures may differ from final published values.
