About Us

Experienced procurement, contracting, and business operations leadership.

With more than 45 years of combined experience, our leadership team specializes in procurement transformation, strategic sourcing, contract management, supplier governance, and technology-enabled business operations. We help organizations optimize procurement functions, reduce costs, improve operational efficiency, and strengthen supplier performance across highly regulated and global industries — including financial services, consulting, hospitality, technology, and government contracting.

Our experience includes managing multi-billion-dollar spend portfolios, leading enterprise-wide sourcing initiatives, implementing modern procurement and contract management technologies, and negotiating complex commercial, technology, software, cloud, and SaaS agreements. We have built and transformed procurement organizations, streamlined processes, enhanced governance frameworks, and delivered measurable cost savings and operational improvements that support long-term business objectives.

In addition to procurement and contracting expertise, we design and deliver executive workshops, procurement training programs, and stakeholder engagement initiatives that help organizations build internal capabilities and drive sustainable change. Our team also brings more than a decade of experience leading large-scale staff augmentation programs for enterprise organizations, helping clients access specialized talent, improve workforce flexibility, and maintain strong governance and cost control.

We're intentionally small. That means senior-only staffing on every engagement — no bench of junior consultants learning on your dime — direct access to the people doing the work, and the flexibility to move at your pace, not a standardized methodology's.
