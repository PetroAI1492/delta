How the Financial Health Score is calculated
Scores are weighted to answer the two questions every donor should ask: Does my dollar reach the mission? and Will this organization still exist in 5 years? Program efficiency carries the most weight (35 pts), followed by financial reserves (25 pts), CEO compensation (15 pts), revenue trend (10 pts), and governance (15 pts).
Note on infrastructure-heavy organizations: Disaster relief, hospital, and blood supply organizations operate extensive physical infrastructure that legitimately increases non-program overhead. A program efficiency score just below 75% reflects operational model, not mismanagement.
Note on declining revenue trend: Many organizations show declining revenue in 2023 vs 2022 — normalization after COVID giving surges. A single year of decline is a watch item, not a red flag, unless paired with low reserves.
Note on Feeding America reserves: 1.5 months of reserves reflects its food distribution model — it moves product continuously with minimal cash buffer by design.
Note on City of Hope reserves: 206 months reflects endowment and long-term investment assets common in academic medical centers — not excess cash on hand.
Note on St. Jude — 100% program efficiency: EIN 35-1044585 belongs to ALSAC, the fundraising arm. Clinical and research expenses sit on the hospital's separate 990 (EIN 62-0646012). Review both 990s together.

Questions worth investigating before donating
Cancer sector
Multiple cancer orgs show declining revenue in 2023. Is this sector-wide donor fatigue following COVID giving surges, or a structural shift? Check 2024 giving trends before drawing conclusions.
Efficiency vs. impact
A high program efficiency ratio measures financial discipline — not real-world outcomes. Two orgs with identical scores may have vastly different impact per dollar. Ask what the org measures as success and whether they publish outcome data.
United Way
United Way's intermediary model has faced structural challenges for years as donors give directly and workplace campaigns shrink. Worth reading their most recent annual report before deciding.
CEO compensation at scale
The compensation flag uses a flat 5% threshold regardless of org size. Always look at comp in the context of total budget, not in isolation.
Unverified program ratios (†)
Several orgs show — † for program efficiency because the ProPublica API did not return reliable structured data. This is a data gap, not a red flag. Download the full 990 from IRS.gov and review Part IX directly.
Recent news matters
990 data lags reality by 12–24 months. Leadership changes, scandals, or major new grants won't appear here yet. Always search recent news for any org you plan to give to.
