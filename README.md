## Products

### 📊 Petroleum Signal Brief
A structured intelligence brief covering global crude and refined product markets. Each edition classifies current geopolitical, supply, transit, infrastructure, and sanctions developments into a quantified pressure score with an AI-enhanced executive summary.

- Pressure score (0–100) with directional regime classification
- Top classified events with severity, direction, category, and timing
- Primary structural node and geographic concentration analysis
- Flow status and chokepoint risk assessment
- AI executive summary calibrated to current market conditions
- Delivered as an encrypted PDF and HTML dashboard

### ⚠️ Energy Alert
A real-time tripwire that monitors global news continuously and fires only when a high-severity tightening event is detected. No noise — the system is silent until something material happens.

- Covers military incidents, chokepoint disruptions, infrastructure attacks, sanctions, and supply shocks
- Fully deterministic classification — no AI inference on the trigger itself
- Structural impact context — volume, exposure, and affected markets
- Delivered immediately via email when triggered
