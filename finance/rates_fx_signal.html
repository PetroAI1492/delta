Methodology: The 2-Year Treasury yield embeds the market's expectation for the average Fed Funds rate over the next two years — it is the cleanest free proxy for "what does the bond market think the Fed will do" without a paid Fed Funds futures feed (CME). When 2Y is meaningfully below the current target range, the market is pricing rate cuts; when at or above, the market agrees with a hold or expects further hikes. Broad Dollar Index (DTWEXBGS) is the Federal Reserve's own trade-weighted measure — not the commercial DXY ticker, but the equivalent Fed-published series.
Cross-reference with Federal Reserve Watch: Compare this report's Market vs Fed signal against the dot plot median projection and current policy stance in the Fed Watch report. Agreement between bond market pricing and FOMC projections suggests policy credibility; divergence suggests the market either doubts the Fed's resolve or expects economic conditions to force a change in path.
