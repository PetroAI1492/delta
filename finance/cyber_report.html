Excess Return vs. S&P 500 — Market-Adjusted Signal

How to read this: Excess return = ticker return minus SPY return over the same ±3 trading-day window. A positive number means the cyber stock outperformed the broad market around that KEV addition — the signal is specific to cybersecurity, not just a rising tide. A negative number means it lagged. This strips out days where everything went up or down together.
