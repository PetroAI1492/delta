How to read this: Bid-to-cover ratio is total bids received divided by amount accepted — higher means stronger demand for that auction. Below ~2.0 on notes/bonds is historically weak. Indirect bidders typically include foreign central banks and large institutional investors purchasing through primary dealers — a rough proxy for foreign demand for US debt. Direct bidders are large domestic institutional investors bidding directly. Primary dealers are obligated to bid and often absorb what the market doesn't want — high primary dealer share can signal soft underlying demand. (BEY) next to a yield indicates a Treasury Bill's bond-equivalent yield (Bills are quoted at discount, not coupon yield — BEY makes them comparable to Notes/Bonds).
