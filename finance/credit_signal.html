★ HY OAS (high yield option-adjusted spread) is the primary credit stress signal — the risk premium investors demand over Treasuries for holding below-investment-grade corporate debt. Widening = market pricing in higher default/recession risk. HY-IG differential isolates risk appetite from rate-level effects — its compression often signals risk-on extremes; its widening signals broad risk aversion. Current HY-IG differential (2.06%) is near historical cycle tights (2007 lows ~2.0%, 2021 ~2.1%) — indicating strong risk appetite. Data restricted to 3-year window per FRED policy (April 2026).
